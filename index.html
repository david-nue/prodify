What's inside

One space.
Every tool you need.

Stop switching between apps. Prodify brings everything into one flexible workspace — your way.

Task Board
Three columns — To Do, In Progress, Done. Drag tasks, set priorities, and see exactly what needs your attention today.
Focus Timer
Set a timer, block out distractions, and track how many sessions you complete each day. Simple but surprisingly effective.
Daily Journal
Write freely, pick a mood, and build a daily reflection habit. Your entries are private, searchable, and always yours.
Calendar
A clean weekly view with your events and task deadlines in one place. Plan your week without hopping between apps.
Habit Tracker
Check off daily habits, pick an emoji, and watch your streak grow. Free plan includes 3 habits — Pro unlocks unlimited. Small wins, every day.
Widget Canvas
Place any tool anywhere on the canvas. Resize it, move it, stack it. Your layout is saved automatically — every time.
Notes
Quick notes that live right inside your workspace. Jot down ideas, meeting notes, or anything you need to capture fast — no separate app needed.
Sticky Notes
Pin colorful sticky notes anywhere on your canvas. Resize them, recolor them, or pin a note straight from your Notes widget — your canvas, your clutter.

Prodify
Your Productivity Space

Work smarter,
live clearer

Tasks, journal, projects & calendar — one flexible workspace built around how you actually work.

On iOS: tap Share then "Add to Home Screen"
Task Board
Kanban columns with drag-and-drop, High/Med/Low priorities, and instant filtering to keep you focused on what matters most.
Focus Timer
Pomodoro-ready with Short Break, Long Break and fully custom durations. Session dots track your progress through each work block.
Project Progress
Track multiple projects with visual progress bars and grade scores. See exactly how far each goal has come at a glance.
Daily Journal
Write entries, tag moods with emoji, and build a habit of daily reflection. Your emotional history is yours to keep and revisit.
Calendar
Weekly view with project-linked events. Plan your week, add events to any day, and see everything together in one clean layout.
Widget Canvas
Arrange every tool freely on a drag-and-resize canvas. Your workspace, your layout — completely flexible and always saved.

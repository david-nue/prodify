What's inside

One space.
Every tool you need.

Stop switching between apps. Prodify brings everything into one flexible workspace — your way.

Task Board
Three columns — To Do, In Progress, Done. Drag tasks, set priorities, filter by project. See exactly what needs your attention today.
Focus Timer
Set a timer, block out distractions, and track how many sessions you complete each day. Simple but surprisingly effective.
Project Tracker
Track every project with progress bars, task lists, and a grade score. Know at a glance what needs attention and what's nearly done.
Daily Journal
Write freely, pick a mood, and build a daily reflection habit. Your entries are private, searchable, and always yours.
Calendar
A clean weekly view with your events and project deadlines in one place. Plan your week without hopping between apps.
Widget Canvas
Place any tool anywhere on the canvas. Resize it, move it, stack it. Your layout is saved automatically — every time.
